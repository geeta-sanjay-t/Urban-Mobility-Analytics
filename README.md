# Urban-Mobility-Analytics
#Project Overview

This project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of Seoul Bike Sharing data to understand how weather conditions, seasons, and time-related factors influence bike rental demand. The analysis explores rental patterns and identifies important business insights that can help improve bike availability and operational planning.

#Business Problem
The objective is to understand hourly bike rental demand and identify the factors that influence customer usage.

#Business Objectives
Analyze hourly and seasonal bike rental patterns.
Identify peak rental hours and high-demand months.
Understand the relationship between weather conditions and bike rentals.
Generate insights to support bike availability and business planning.

#Key Findings
Bike rental demand is higher during peak commuting hours.
Summer shows higher rental demand than winter.
Weather conditions influence rental patterns.
Temperature and dew point show positive relationships with bike rental demand.
Rental activity varies across weekdays, months and seasons.

#Business Recommendations
Maintain sufficient bike availability during peak commuting hours.
Optimize bike distribution during high-demand seasons.
Consider weather conditions when planning bike inventory.
Use historical rental trends for operational planning.
